# 🪔 Festive Season Sales & Procurement Executive Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI executive suite designed to evaluate festive season sales velocity, procurement efficiency, buyer/supplier concentration, and commercial discount impact across **INR 115+ Cr** in combined commercial volume.

---

### 📈 1. Festive Sales Executive Comparison
![Festive Sales Dashboard](Fetive%20Sales%20Analysis.png)

- **Revenue & Volume Performance:** Closed **INR 56.65 Cr** across **9,197 units** (+18.3% YoY growth vs 2024) with an average realization of INR 61,600/unit.
- **Dynamic Target Simulation:** Built what-if growth parameter slicers (+20% growth plan) to project 2026 revenue (INR 67.98 Cr) and unit targets.
- **Automated Executive Summary:** Dynamic DAX measure generating instant natural-language takeaways on top buyers and best-selling SKUs.

---

### 🛒 2. Festive Purchase & Procurement Comparison
![Festive Purchase Dashboard](Festive%20Purchase%20Analysis.png)

- **Procurement & Discount Tracking:** Monitored **INR 58.97 Cr** gross procurement across **9,303 units**, capturing **INR 3.51 Cr** in commercial discounts (Net purchase: INR 55.46 Cr).
- **Supplier Concentration Analysis:** Evaluated top supplier share (top supplier contributing 27.2% of volume) and model-level landed cost efficiency.

---

### 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
- **Power BI Desktop:** Multi-page executive reporting, KPI cards, dynamic DAX text summaries, and What-If parameters.
- **Power Query & Excel:** Automated data cleaning, SKU standardization, and multi-party reconciliation.
